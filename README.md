# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Portfolio

## About Me

My path into cybersecurity started in the United States Air Force, where I began my career in IT. Over time, I found myself drawn more and more to the security side of things — understanding how systems could be broken, and more importantly, how to protect them. That curiosity drove me to deliberately steer my career toward security, building my skills from the ground up through real-world experience. I've now been working as a Security Engineer for 7 years, and the field still challenges and motivates me every day.

---

## CTF Experience

The National Cyber League (NCL) Individual CTF was my first formal Capture the Flag competition followed by the NCL Team game where I was the captain of the SANS.edu Accidental Threat Actors, which achived 45th place out of 342 teams in the Experienced Student Bracket Spring 2026 . Coming from a professional security background, I was curious how my real-world skills would translate into a structured competition environment. The experience reinforced fundamentals I use on the job while also exposing me to new tools, techniques, and ways of thinking through problems methodically under pressure.
